Overview

What Is a Free Immigration Eligibility Assessment?

A free immigration eligibility assessment is an initial review of your personal profile to identify visa or immigration pathways that may suit you. Sky Quest evaluates factors such as your age, education, work experience, language ability, immigration history, and destination preferences, then matches your profile with potentially relevant programs and explains the next steps.

Assessment Factors

What We Check During Your Immigration Assessment

Every immigration pathway has different eligibility rules. We review the main factors in your profile to understand which visa or immigration options may be relevant to you.

Age

Age can influence eligibility and points under certain skilled migration and permanent residence programs. We consider how your age fits the requirements of the pathway being assessed.

Education

We review your degree, diploma, field of study, specialization, and other qualifications to understand whether your academic background supports your chosen immigration route.

Occupation and Work Experience

Your current job, occupation, years of experience, and employment history can be important for skilled migration and work-based programs. We assess how your professional background may align with available pathways.

Language Proficiency

Some immigration programs require proof of English or another language. Where relevant, we consider your language ability and any recognized test results as part of the assessment.

Nationality and Qatar Residency Status

Your nationality and current residence in Qatar can affect application procedures, documentation, and available visa routes. Qatar residency itself does not automatically determine immigration eligibility.

Marital and Family Status

For certain programs, your spouse, children, or other family circumstances may affect points, dependent eligibility, sponsorship options, or supporting-document requirements.

Previous Visa and Immigration History

We review previous visas, refusals, overstays, and other immigration matters that may be relevant to a future application.

Destination and Immigration Goal

Finally, we consider what you want to achieve, whether that is study, work, permanent residence, business, or family migration, and match your profile with potentially suitable pathways.

Australia

Australia Skilled Migration Eligibility Assessment

If you are living in Qatar and considering skilled migration to Australia, an eligibility assessment can help you understand whether your profile may fit a points-tested pathway.

We review factors such as your age, nominated occupation, qualifications, English ability, and skilled work experience. For points-tested visas such as subclasses 189, 190 and 491, Australia uses SkillSelect and awards points for factors including age, education, English proficiency and employment experience. Meeting the minimum points threshold does not guarantee an invitation to apply.

Your occupation can also matter because some skilled visa pathways require it to appear on an eligible skilled occupation list, along with a suitable skills assessment from the relevant assessing authority.

Sky Quest can help you understand your initial profile, possible points-based pathways, and the next steps before you proceed.

Important

Does an Eligibility Assessment Guarantee a Visa?

No. An eligibility assessment only indicates whether your profile may fit certain visa or immigration pathways.

It is different from a formal visa application. The final decision is always made by the relevant immigration authority, embassy, consulate, or government department after reviewing the complete application and supporting documents.
