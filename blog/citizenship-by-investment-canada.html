Want to Get a Second Passport? Know About Citizenship by Investment in Canada (2026)

Can you get Canadian citizenship by investment in 2026? Learn how investor immigration, Quebec's program, PR and citizenship rules really work.

Canada Immigration • Investor Visa • Second Passport

A second passport can be attractive for many reasons. You may want to:

  • Build a future for your family
  • Expand your business internationally
  • Live in another country
  • Access new career opportunities
  • Give your children more options
  • Become a permanent resident abroad
  • Eventually qualify for another citizenship

This is why people often search for terms such as Citizenship by Investment in Canada, Canada investor visa, Canadian second passport, or Canada PR by investment.

But there is one very important fact to understand first:

Canada does not currently have a direct program where you invest a fixed amount of money and immediately receive Canadian citizenship or a Canadian passport.

Canada's former federal Immigrant Investor Program and federal Entrepreneur Program were terminated in 2014. Current Canadian immigration options for business people and investors focus instead on routes such as Quebec business immigration and certain Provincial Nominee Program pathways.

In simple terms:

Investment or business immigration → Permanent Residence → Meet citizenship requirements → Canadian citizenship → Canadian passport

It is a process, not an instant passport purchase.

This guide explains how citizenship by investment in Canada really works in 2026, which investor and entrepreneur options remain relevant, how the Quebec Investor Program works, and what you must eventually do to qualify for Canadian citizenship.

Does Canada Have Citizenship by Investment in 2026?

No, Canada does not currently offer direct citizenship by investment.

You cannot simply invest CAD $500,000, CAD $1 million, buy a house, or purchase a Canadian business and automatically become a Canadian citizen.

Canada did operate a federal Immigrant Investor Program in the past, but the federal government terminated that program in 2014.

Today, business people may instead explore immigration routes that can lead to Canadian permanent residence.

After becoming a permanent resident, they must separately meet Canada's citizenship requirements before they can apply to become Canadian citizens.

That distinction is extremely important.

Citizenship by Investment vs Investment Immigration

These two phrases sound similar, but they mean different things.

Direct Citizenship by Investment

Under a true citizenship-by-investment model, an eligible investor contributes or invests a required amount and may receive citizenship after completing the country's due-diligence process.

Canada does not currently operate this type of federal citizenship program.

Investment or Business Immigration

Investment immigration normally means using an eligible business or investor immigration program to work toward:

  1. Provincial selection or another immigration status
  2. Permanent residence
  3. Residence in Canada
  4. Citizenship eligibility later

This is much closer to how Canada's current system works.

Can Investment Eventually Lead to a Canadian Passport?

Potentially, yes, but not automatically.

Think of the process as four separate stages:

Stage 1: Qualify for an immigration program

You first need to qualify under an available Canadian investor, business or entrepreneur immigration pathway.

Stage 2: Obtain permanent residence

Depending on the program, there can be provincial and federal immigration stages before you become a permanent resident.

For example, Quebec business-class applicants must first obtain a Certificat de sélection du Québec, or CSQ, before applying to the federal government for permanent residence.

Stage 3: Live in Canada and become eligible for citizenship

Adult citizenship applicants generally need to be physically present in Canada for at least 1,095 days during the five-year eligibility period before applying.

Stage 4: Become a citizen and apply for a passport

After your citizenship application is approved and you complete the citizenship process, you receive proof of Canadian citizenship.

Only after obtaining your Canadian citizenship certificate can you apply for a Canadian passport.

So the investment itself does not give you a Canadian passport.

What Investment Immigration Options Does Canada Have in 2026?

Canada's business-immigration landscape has changed significantly.

For investors researching Canada in 2026, three areas are particularly important:

  1. Quebec Investor Program
  2. Provincial Nominee Program entrepreneur or business streams
  3. Start-Up Visa Program, which is currently paused for new applications

Let's understand each one.

1. Quebec Immigrant Investor Program

The Quebec Investor Program is one of the closest Canadian options to what people commonly call an "investor visa."

However, it is still not direct citizenship by investment.

The program is designed for investors who intend to settle in Quebec and meet specific financial, management, education, French-language and residence requirements.

Quebec Investor Program Requirements in 2026

According to the Government of Quebec, applicants need to satisfy several conditions.

Minimum Net Worth: CAD $2 Million

The principal applicant must have a net worth of at least:

CAD $2,000,000

The amount can include qualifying assets shared with a spouse or common-law partner included in the application.

Applicants must also demonstrate the lawful origin and accumulation of their wealth.

This means the government is not simply interested in seeing a large bank balance. You need to be able to explain where the money came from.

That could involve evidence relating to:

  • Business ownership
  • Employment income
  • Investments
  • Real estate
  • Inheritance
  • Company profits
  • Other lawful sources of wealth

The evidence required depends on the individual's financial history.

Management Experience Requirement

Applicants must have at least two years of qualifying management experience acquired during the five years before submitting the application.

This makes the program different from a simple passive investment product. An applicant's professional and management background matters.

Education Requirement

Applicants must also meet Quebec's education requirement.

The program requires at least a qualifying secondary-level or vocational/college credential as defined by Quebec's immigration rules.

French Language Requirement

This is one of the most important modern requirements.

An investor must demonstrate spoken French at a minimum of Level 7 on Quebec's French proficiency scale.

The Quebec government accepts designated French tests and qualifying evidence, and test results generally must be less than two years old when the application is submitted.

This means a wealthy investor who does not meet the French requirement should not assume that having sufficient money alone makes them eligible.

How Much Must You Invest in Quebec?

Under the current Quebec Investor Program, the financial intermediary must make:

CAD $1,000,000 as a five-year term investment

plus:

CAD $200,000 as a financial contribution

to Investissement Québec Immigrants Investisseurs Inc. when requested by the ministry.

The CAD $1 million investment is guaranteed by the Government of Quebec and is returned without interest at the end of the investment period under the program's rules.

The separate CAD $200,000 contribution is a contribution rather than part of the refundable CAD $1 million investment.

Simple Example

Imagine an investor named Ahmed.

Ahmed has:

  • More than CAD $2 million in qualifying net worth
  • More than two years of recent management experience
  • The required education
  • The required French level
  • A genuine intention to settle in Quebec

If he satisfies the program requirements, his authorized financial intermediary would arrange the required investment and contribution when Quebec requests them.

This still does not make Ahmed a Canadian citizen.

There are more immigration and residence steps first.

Quebec Residence Requirement for Investors

The Quebec Investor Program also includes a real residence requirement.

After the relevant work permit is issued, the applicant must complete at least 12 months of residence in Quebec within two years.

At least six months must be completed by the principal applicant. The remaining six months can be completed by the principal applicant or their spouse or de facto spouse. This is another reason the program should not be described as a "passport for money" scheme.

The applicant needs a genuine connection with Quebec.

How Does the Quebec Investor Route Work?

A simplified version looks like this:

  1. Check eligibility
  2. Enter into the required investment agreement with an authorized financial intermediary
  3. Submit the Quebec immigration application
  4. Provide evidence of net worth, management experience, education and French ability
  5. Complete the required investment and contribution when requested
  6. Receive a Notice of Intent to Select if the applicable conditions are met
  7. Apply for the relevant work permit
  8. Complete Quebec's residence requirement
  9. Obtain a Quebec Selection Certificate when the requirements are satisfied
  10. Apply federally for Canadian permanent residence
  11. Meet Canada's citizenship requirements after becoming a permanent resident
  12. Apply for Canadian citizenship
  13. After becoming a citizen, apply for a Canadian passport

Quebec handles provincial selection, while the federal Government of Canada handles the permanent residence stage.

2. Provincial Entrepreneur and Business Immigration

Quebec is not the only place where business people may find immigration opportunities.

Canada's Provincial Nominee Program, or PNP, allows participating provinces and territories to nominate people who can contribute to their economies.

This can include business people as well as skilled workers.

The Government of Canada confirms that provinces can operate streams designed to recruit and retain entrepreneurs and investors, but each province or territory sets its own requirements.

Depending on the province, requirements may involve factors such as:

  • Business ownership
  • Management experience
  • Minimum personal net worth
  • Minimum investment
  • Business plan
  • Job creation
  • Active management
  • Language ability
  • Exploratory visits
  • Residence in the province

These requirements are program-specific and can change.

Important Difference

Many provincial entrepreneur programs are active-business programs.

That means the applicant may need to:

  1. Establish or purchase a business
  2. Operate it actively
  3. Meet performance conditions
  4. Create economic benefits
  5. Obtain provincial nomination
  6. Apply for permanent residence

This is very different from simply transferring money into an account.

The federal government describes the PNP as a route for people who want to live in the nominating province or territory and become permanent residents.

3. Canada Start-Up Visa Program in 2026

The Start-Up Visa Program used to be another major federal business immigration option.

However, this is where a lot of outdated online content can mislead readers.

Is the Start-Up Visa Open in 2026?

Not for normal new applications as of August 2026.

The Government of Canada states that the Start-Up Visa Program was paused on June 30, 2026.

Canada is continuing to process qualifying applications accepted before the deadline, but the program is currently closed to other new applications.

Applicants with a valid 2025 commitment certificate had to apply by June 30, 2026.

IRCC now identifies the program's status as Paused.

This matters because many old articles still describe the Start-Up Visa as if anyone can begin a new application today.

That information is no longer current.

Is Buying Property in Canada Enough to Get Citizenship?

No.

Buying a house, apartment, commercial property or other Canadian asset does not by itself make someone a Canadian citizen.

Canadian citizenship requires qualifying immigration status and meeting citizenship eligibility rules. Adult applicants must first have permanent resident status and satisfy Canada's citizenship requirements.

Similarly:

Buying property ≠ Canadian PR

and

Buying property ≠ Canadian citizenship

Do not confuse a normal investment with an immigration program.

Does Buying a Canadian Business Give You Citizenship?

Not automatically.

Purchasing or establishing a business may form part of certain provincial entrepreneur immigration strategies, depending on the relevant program.

But ownership of a Canadian company by itself does not grant citizenship.

Provincial business immigration programs have eligibility, nomination and immigration requirements that must be completed separately.

How Do You Actually Become a Canadian Citizen?

Once you become a Canadian permanent resident, citizenship becomes a separate process.

For a normal adult citizenship application in 2026, several major requirements apply.

1. You Must Be a Permanent Resident

Adult citizenship applicants must have Canadian permanent resident status.

Investment alone does not replace this requirement.

2. You Need Enough Physical Presence in Canada

You generally need to have been physically present in Canada for at least:

1,095 days during the five years before applying for citizenship.

That equals approximately three years.

The five-year eligibility period must include at least 730 days as a permanent resident.

Some qualifying time spent in Canada before becoming a permanent resident may count at half-day value, subject to Canada's rules and limits.

3. You May Need to File Canadian Taxes

Citizenship applicants may need to have filed Canadian income taxes for at least three years during the relevant five-year period if they were required to file.

Immigration status and tax residence are not identical concepts, so anyone with significant international assets or businesses should obtain appropriate tax advice for their circumstances.

4. You May Need to Prove English or French Ability

Adults between 18 and 54 years old generally need to demonstrate adequate English or French ability.

Canada describes the required level as approximately Canadian Language Benchmark Level 4 for English or NCLC 4 for French.

5. You May Need to Pass the Citizenship Test

Applicants aged 18 to 54 generally need to take the Canadian citizenship test.

The test covers topics such as:

  • Canadian history
  • Geography
  • Government
  • Laws
  • Economy
  • Rights and responsibilities
  • Canadian symbols

6. You Must Take the Citizenship Oath

Once the citizenship application is approved, eligible adult applicants must complete the citizenship ceremony and take the oath of citizenship.

Only after becoming a citizen can you proceed to obtain your Canadian passport.

How Much Does Canadian Citizenship Cost in 2026?

As of August 2026, the Government of Canada lists the adult Canadian citizenship application fee as:

CAD $653

This includes the processing fee and right of citizenship fee.

The fee for a minor citizenship application is currently:

CAD $100.

These are citizenship fees only.

They do not include the much larger investments, immigration processing fees, professional expenses or other costs connected with an investor immigration pathway.

Permanent Residence Is Not the Same as Citizenship

This distinction causes a lot of confusion.

Permanent Resident

A Canadian permanent resident can generally live, work and study in Canada, subject to Canadian law and their status.

To maintain PR status, a permanent resident normally must be physically present in Canada for at least 730 days during the previous five years, although limited exceptions can allow certain time abroad to count.

Canadian Citizen

A citizen has Canadian citizenship rather than permanent resident status and can apply for a Canadian passport.

Citizenship requires a separate application and eligibility process.

So:

PR comes before citizenship for most investor immigrants.

Can Canada Be Your Second Citizenship?

Canada permits dual citizenship under Canadian law.

This means Canada does not generally require a person to give up another nationality simply because they become Canadian.

However, there is an important warning:

Your current country may have different rules.

Canada specifically advises prospective citizens to check the citizenship laws of their other country because some countries may not allow citizens to keep their original nationality after acquiring Canadian citizenship.

Therefore, before planning to obtain a Canadian "second passport," check the rules governing your existing citizenship.

When Do You Actually Get a Canadian Passport?

You do not receive a Canadian passport when you:

  • Invest money
  • Receive a work permit
  • Receive provincial nomination
  • Become a permanent resident

You first need to become a Canadian citizen.

After completing the citizenship process, you receive a citizenship certificate.

The Government of Canada states that you can apply for a Canadian passport only after receiving proof of Canadian citizenship.

The simplified journey is therefore:

Investor/business pathway

↓

Work permit or provincial stage where applicable

↓

Permanent residence

↓

Physical presence and citizenship eligibility

↓

Canadian citizenship application

↓

Citizenship ceremony

↓

Citizenship certificate

↓

Canadian passport application

Citizenship by Investment Canada: How Long Does It Take?

There is no honest answer such as:

"Invest today and get a Canadian passport in three years."

That would oversimplify the system.

The total timeline depends on:

  • Immigration program
  • Provincial processing
  • Federal permanent residence processing
  • Background checks
  • Medical and security checks
  • Residence requirements
  • How much time you physically spend in Canada
  • Citizenship processing
  • Individual circumstances

Even after obtaining PR, the citizenship physical-presence requirement generally requires 1,095 qualifying days during the previous five years.

Therefore, Canadian citizenship should be treated as a long-term immigration objective, not an immediate investment product.

Citizenship by Investment Canada: How Much Does It Cost?

There is no single "Canada citizenship by investment price" because Canada does not sell citizenship for a fixed investment.

If you are considering the Quebec Investor Program, the current program includes:

Requirement Current Amount
Minimum net worth CAD $2,000,000
Five-year investment CAD $1,000,000
Financial contribution CAD $200,000
Adult citizenship application later CAD $653

The Quebec investment and contribution requirements are current as of the province's August 6, 2026 update.

Other costs may arise during the immigration process and will depend on the individual case.

Can Your Family Be Included?

Canadian permanent residence applications can generally include eligible accompanying family members when they meet the applicable dependent-family rules.

IRCC identifies family members who may be processed as dependants as including eligible:

  • Spouses
  • Common-law partners
  • Dependent children

The exact requirements should always be checked before filing because family composition and dependency rules matter.

Is Citizenship by Investment the Same as a Golden Visa?

Not exactly.

The phrase Golden Visa is commonly used for residence-by-investment schemes in some countries.

Canada does not officially market a federal "Golden Visa" giving residence or citizenship simply for buying real estate or making a passive investment.

Canada's current official immigration system instead includes provincial and Quebec business immigration pathways with their own requirements.

Who Might Consider Canadian Investor Immigration?

Canada's investor or entrepreneur pathways may be worth researching if you are:

  • A business owner
  • A senior manager
  • An entrepreneur
  • A high-net-worth individual
  • Interested in settling in Canada
  • Willing to establish genuine residence
  • Prepared to meet language requirements where applicable
  • Able to demonstrate the lawful source of your assets
  • Looking for permanent residence rather than an instant passport

The important phrase here is settling in Canada.

If your only objective is to invest money and receive a passport without actually establishing a life in the country, Canada's current system is not designed as a simple direct citizenship-by-investment program.

Who May Not Be Suitable for the Quebec Investor Program?

The Quebec investor route may not fit everyone.

For example, it may not suit someone who:

  • Cannot demonstrate CAD $2 million in qualifying net worth
  • Cannot prove the lawful origin of their wealth
  • Lacks the required recent management experience
  • Does not satisfy the education criteria
  • Cannot meet the required French-speaking level
  • Does not intend to establish the required presence in Quebec

These are substantive program conditions, not optional recommendations.

Common Myths About Canadian Citizenship by Investment

Myth 1: Invest CAD $1 Million and Get a Canadian Passport

False.

The Quebec Investor Program includes a CAD $1 million term investment, but that investment is part of an immigration process.

It does not directly purchase Canadian citizenship.

Myth 2: Buying a House Gives You Canadian PR

False.

Property ownership by itself is not a Canadian permanent residence program.

Myth 3: Canada Still Has the Old Federal Investor Visa

False.

The old federal Immigrant Investor Program was terminated in 2014.

Myth 4: Start-Up Visa Is Open to New Applicants in 2026

Currently false.

The Start-Up Visa Program was paused on June 30, 2026 and is not accepting normal new applications.

Myth 5: Permanent Residence Automatically Turns Into Citizenship

False.

A permanent resident must separately qualify and apply for citizenship.

Myth 6: A Consultant Can Guarantee Canadian Citizenship

False.

Immigration and citizenship decisions are made by Canadian government authorities.

A consultant may assist with understanding a process and preparing documents, but no private company can legitimately promise that it controls the government's final decision.

Frequently Asked Questions About Citizenship by Investment in Canada

Does Canada offer citizenship by investment in 2026?

No direct citizenship-by-investment program currently allows someone to simply make an investment and automatically receive Canadian citizenship.

Canadian business immigration may provide pathways toward permanent residence, after which normal citizenship requirements must still be met.

Can I get Canadian citizenship by investing money?

An investment alone does not grant Canadian citizenship.

An eligible business or investor immigration pathway may eventually lead to permanent residence, and a permanent resident may later apply for citizenship after meeting Canada's requirements.

How much do I need to invest in Canada to get PR?

There is no one universal investment amount for Canadian permanent residence.

For the Quebec Investor Program, the current requirements include a CAD $1 million five-year investment and CAD $200,000 financial contribution, together with other eligibility criteria. Other provincial business programs set their own conditions.

How much net worth is required for the Quebec Investor Program?

The current minimum is CAD $2 million, with the lawful origin and accumulation of wealth needing to be demonstrated.

Is French compulsory for the Quebec Investor Program?

Under the current rules, applicants need spoken French equivalent to at least Level 7 on Quebec's French proficiency scale.

Is the CAD $1 million Quebec investment refundable?

The Quebec government states that the CAD $1 million five-year investment is government-guaranteed and returned without interest after the investment period according to the program rules.

The separate CAD $200,000 amount is a financial contribution.

Can I get a Canadian passport immediately after PR?

No.

Permanent residence and citizenship are different statuses.

You must first meet Canada's citizenship eligibility requirements and become a Canadian citizen before applying for a Canadian passport.

How many years do I need to live in Canada before citizenship?

Adult applicants generally need 1,095 days of physical presence during the five years immediately before applying, subject to Canada's detailed calculation rules.

Does Canada allow dual citizenship?

Yes, Canada allows dual citizenship under Canadian law.

However, another country may not allow you to retain its citizenship after becoming Canadian, so you should check the rules of your existing nationality.

Is Canada's Start-Up Visa open in 2026?

The program is currently paused.

IRCC stopped accepting normal new Start-Up Visa applications on June 30, 2026, although qualifying applications already received continue to be processed.

Can buying property in Canada give me citizenship?

No.

Buying property does not replace the permanent residence and citizenship requirements established by Canadian immigration law.
