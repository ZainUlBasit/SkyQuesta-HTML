Planning to visit Canada from Qatar in 2026?

Before collecting documents or paying a visa fee, there is one important question to answer:

What passport do you hold?

Living in Qatar does not automatically determine whether you need a Canadian visitor visa. Canada's entry requirements are mainly based on your citizenship, passport, purpose of travel and method of travel.

There was also an important rule change for Qatari citizens.

As of November 25, 2025, Canada removed the visitor visa requirement for citizens of Qatar. Qatari passport holders travelling to Canada by air now normally need an Electronic Travel Authorization, or eTA, instead of a Temporary Resident Visa.

However, a person who lives in Qatar but holds another country's passport may still need a Canada visitor visa, officially known as a Temporary Resident Visa or TRV.

This guide explains both situations.

Important 2026 Update for Qatari Citizens

This is the biggest change Qatar-based applicants need to know.

Canada officially lifted the visitor visa requirement for Qatari citizens on November 25, 2025.

Therefore, if you hold a Qatari passport and are travelling to Canada by air, you normally do not need to go through the traditional Canada visitor visa application process.

Instead, you normally apply for an eTA.

The eTA is electronically connected to your passport. It is not a visa sticker placed inside your passport.

How much does a Canadian eTA cost in 2026?

The official Government of Canada eTA fee is:

CAN$7

The fee is non-refundable.

Be careful with third-party websites charging much higher amounts. IRCC confirms that the official Government of Canada eTA costs only CAN$7.

What do Qatari citizens need to apply for an eTA?

You generally need:

  • A valid Qatari passport
  • A valid email address
  • An accepted credit or debit card
  • Internet access
  • The information requested in the online application

The Government of Canada states that most eTA applications are approved within minutes, although some applications can take longer if additional information or documents are required.

How long is a Canadian eTA valid?

An approved eTA can normally remain valid for up to five years or until the passport linked to it expires, whichever happens first.

If you obtain a new passport, you generally need a new eTA.

A valid eTA can normally be used for multiple trips while it remains valid.

What if You Live in Qatar but Are Not a Qatari Citizen?

This is where many applicants become confused.

A Qatar Residence Permit or Qatar ID does not automatically give you the same Canadian entry requirements as a Qatari citizen.

For example, a person may:

  • Live in Doha
  • Hold a Qatar ID
  • Work for a company in Qatar

but still travel using an Indian, Pakistani, Philippine, Nepalese, Bangladeshi or another country's passport.

Canada will consider the passport and citizenship being used for the trip.

Before applying, use Canada's official visa or eTA eligibility checker to identify the correct travel document.

If your nationality requires a visitor visa, you can normally apply for a Canadian Temporary Resident Visa while legally residing in Qatar.

What Is a Canada Visitor Visa?

A Canadian visitor visa is officially called a Temporary Resident Visa or TRV.

It is placed inside your passport and shows that you met the requirements to travel to Canada as a temporary resident at the time your application was assessed.

A visitor visa may be used for purposes such as:

  • Tourism
  • Visiting relatives
  • Visiting friends
  • Certain short business activities
  • Short-term visits
  • Some short courses
  • Other permitted temporary visits

A visitor visa does not automatically give you permission to work in Canada.

Most foreign nationals who want to work temporarily in Canada need a work permit unless a specific exemption applies.

Canada Visitor Visa Eligibility Requirements in 2026

According to Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada, an applicant generally needs to demonstrate that they meet Canada's temporary-entry requirements.

You should be prepared to show that you:

  • Have a valid passport or other acceptable travel document
  • Are in good health
  • Do not have relevant criminal or immigration-related convictions
  • Have sufficient money for your stay
  • Have reasons to return after your visit
  • Will leave Canada when your authorized stay ends

IRCC specifically considers ties such as employment, a home, financial assets or family connections when assessing whether an applicant is likely to leave Canada after the visit.

This means a Canadian visitor visa application is not simply a document-collection exercise. Your documents should make the purpose and temporary nature of your trip understandable.

Documents Required for a Canada Visa From Qatar

There is no single document list that applies identically to every applicant. When you apply online, IRCC creates a personalized document checklist based on the information you provide.

1. Valid Passport

You need a valid passport.

Your application may require copies of:

  • Passport identity page
  • Previous visas
  • Entry and exit stamps
  • Relevant previous passports or travel history

Your passport validity can also affect how long a visitor visa is issued.

2. Proof of Legal Residence in Qatar

If you are not a Qatari citizen but are applying while residing in Qatar, be prepared to provide evidence of your legal residence when requested.

Depending on your circumstances, this can include your:

  • Qatar ID
  • Residence permit
  • Other evidence of lawful status

3. Travel History

Previous travel can help IRCC understand your immigration and travel history.

Your supporting documents may include copies of:

  • Previous visas
  • Entry stamps
  • Exit stamps
  • Previous passports
  • Previous Canadian visas, if applicable

4. Travel Itinerary

Your application should clearly explain why you want to visit Canada.

IRCC lists itinerary evidence such as:

  • Flight information
  • Accommodation
  • Event registration

as examples of documents that can help establish what you plan to do and how long you expect to stay.

You do not need to create an unrealistic or complicated itinerary. It should simply make sense.

For example: Doha → Toronto → Niagara Falls → Ottawa → Doha may be easier to understand than an unexplained month-long trip covering many provinces without clear accommodation or financial evidence.

5. Proof of Funds

You need enough money to support yourself during your stay.

IRCC does not publish one universal bank-balance number that guarantees approval for every normal visitor visa application.

The amount required depends on factors such as:

  • Length of stay
  • Number of travellers
  • Accommodation
  • Transportation
  • Who is paying
  • Purpose of visit

IRCC specifically identifies bank statements as evidence that can help demonstrate the applicant has sufficient money for the trip.

A financial file can potentially include:

  • Bank statements
  • Salary evidence
  • Employment evidence
  • Savings
  • Sponsor evidence where relevant

Do not borrow money temporarily simply to create an artificial balance. Your overall financial situation should be understandable and consistent with the trip you are proposing.

6. Employment Evidence From Qatar

If you work in Qatar, employment documents can help explain both your financial circumstances and your connection to Qatar.

Depending on your situation, useful documents may include:

  • Employment certificate
  • Salary certificate
  • Approved leave letter
  • Employment contract

7. Invitation Letter, If Applicable

If you are visiting relatives or friends in Canada, an invitation letter may be relevant.

The letter can explain matters such as:

  • Who is inviting you
  • Your relationship
  • Why you are travelling
  • Where you will stay
  • How long you expect to remain in Canada
  • Whether the host is providing financial or accommodation support

However, IRCC explicitly states that an invitation letter does not guarantee visa approval. The applicant still needs to satisfy Canada's visitor requirements.

8. Family Information

Depending on your application method and checklist, adults applying outside Canada may be required to provide a Family Information form.

IRCC states that Family Information forms such as IMM 5645 or IMM 5707 are completed by applicants aged 18 or older when required by their application package.

How to Apply for a Canada Visitor Visa From Qatar in 2026

Here is the process step by step.

Step 1: Check Whether You Need a Visa or eTA

Do this before anything else.

If you are a Qatari citizen travelling by air, you will normally need an eTA rather than a visitor visa under the rules introduced in November 2025.

If you hold another passport, check the Canadian government's entry-document tool.

Do not assume that Qatar residency determines the answer.

Step 2: Determine the Purpose of Your Visit

The purpose of the trip affects what evidence you need.

Common purposes include:

  • Tourism
  • Family visit
  • Business visit
  • Short-term study
  • Medical visit
  • Other temporary travel

Your application should consistently reflect one genuine purpose.

Step 3: Start the Application Online

Canada allows visitor visa applications through its online immigration system.

Applicants using the IRCC Portal answer online questions and upload documents based on their individual circumstances.

Follow the instructions generated by the system you actually use.

Step 4: Complete the Application Carefully

Enter information accurately.

Your application may ask about:

  • Identity
  • Passport
  • Nationality
  • Residence
  • Family
  • Employment
  • Travel history
  • Previous immigration applications
  • Purpose of travel
  • Planned dates
  • Background information

Do not guess when answering important questions.

Your forms and documents should tell the same story.

Step 5: Upload Supporting Documents

The portal will generate the required document checklist.

Upload documents that are clear, readable, complete, relevant and consistent with the application.

IRCC warns that incomplete applications can be refused.

Step 6: Pay the Canada Visitor Visa Fee

As of August 2026, the standard visitor visa application fee starts at:

CAN$100 per applicant.

For a family of five or more eligible members applying together, IRCC lists a maximum family visitor visa fee of CAN$500 under the applicable conditions.

Biometrics may cost extra.

Canada Biometrics Fee in 2026

The current biometrics fee is:

  • CAN$85 for one person
  • Maximum CAN$170 for an eligible family applying together

VFS service charges or optional services are separate from Canadian government application fees and can change.

Step 7: Receive Your Biometric Instruction Letter

If biometrics are required, IRCC will normally issue a Biometric Instruction Letter, or BIL, after your application.

IRCC states that applicants normally have 30 days from receiving the BIL to provide biometrics, subject to the instructions in the individual letter.

Step 8: Book Biometrics at the Canada Visa Application Centre in Doha

Canada has a Visa Application Centre in Doha operated by VFS Global.

As of August 2026, VFS lists the Canada VAC at:

Al Nuaimi Building, Airport Road, Zone 45, Street 310, Doha, Qatar, near Oqba Bin Nafea Metro Station.

VFS currently describes the process as:

  1. Submit the IRCC application
  2. Receive the BIL
  3. Book an appointment
  4. Attend the VAC
  5. Provide biometrics

Booking a biometrics appointment itself is free, although other VAC services may have charges.

Step 9: Wait for IRCC to Process the Application

There is no reliable permanent answer to: "How many days does a Canada visitor visa take from Qatar?"

IRCC processing times change.

Canada's current guidance states that visitor visa processing depends on factors including:

  • Whether the application is complete
  • How easily information can be verified
  • How quickly requested documents are submitted
  • Security screening
  • Application volumes
  • The visa office handling the case

Applicants should check Canada's live processing-time tool immediately before applying.

Step 10: Respond to Additional IRCC Requests

IRCC may ask for additional information such as:

  • More supporting documents
  • Interview
  • Medical examination
  • Police certificate

Follow the instructions and deadline given by IRCC.

Do You Need a Medical Examination for a Canada Visitor Visa?

For a normal visit of six months or less, applicants generally do not need an immigration medical examination.

For stays longer than six months, medical requirements can depend on your travel or residence history and other factors.

If IRCC requires an examination, follow the instructions provided.

Step 11: Receive the Visa Decision

If IRCC approves an online visitor visa application, you will normally receive instructions through your IRCC account asking you to submit your passport.

Approval of the application is not the same thing as already having the visa in your passport. IRCC specifically states that an approved online applicant cannot travel until the required visa has been placed in the passport.

Step 12: Submit Your Passport in Doha

After receiving a passport request, follow the official instructions for passport submission.

The Canada Visa Application Centre can securely transmit passports and documents as part of its authorized administrative services.

VFS does not decide whether your visa is approved. The immigration decision is made by Canadian authorities.

How Long Is a Canada Visitor Visa Valid?

A Canadian visitor visa can be issued as either:

  • Single entry
  • Multiple entry

IRCC states that a visitor visa can generally be valid for up to 10 years, or until the expiry of the passport or biometrics, whichever occurs first.

This does not mean you can remain in Canada for 10 years.

Visa validity and permitted length of stay are two different things.

How Long Can You Stay in Canada as a Visitor?

Most visitors are normally allowed to remain for up to six months.

However, a Canadian border services officer can authorize a shorter or longer stay depending on the situation.

Always follow the actual period granted when you enter Canada.

Does a Canada Visitor Visa Guarantee Entry?

No.

Having a valid visitor visa or eTA allows you to travel to Canada and request entry. The final admission decision is made at the Canadian border.

IRCC states that visitors must continue meeting Canada's entry requirements when they arrive.

Can Qatar Residents Apply for a Canada Visa With Their Family?

Yes, family members can apply at the same time, but each person is still an individual applicant.

IRCC states that family members must complete their own required applications and meet Canada's temporary-residence requirements.

Each person's circumstances are assessed.

Can You Work in Canada on a Visitor Visa?

Normally, a visitor visa is not a work permit.

If your intention is to work temporarily in Canada, you usually need the appropriate work authorization or work permit, unless a specific exemption applies.

Do not apply for a visitor visa while hiding an actual employment purpose.

Can You Study in Canada on a Visitor Visa?

IRCC states that a course or study program lasting six months or less may not require a study permit.

For studies longer than six months, a study permit is generally required.

Your entry document and study authorization are separate concepts.

Canada Visa From Qatar for Previous Canadian or US Visa Holders

Some travellers who would normally come from visa-required countries may qualify for an eTA instead of a visitor visa when travelling to Canada by air.

Eligibility can depend on factors such as having held a Canadian visitor visa within the previous 10 years, or a valid US non-immigrant visa, and meeting Canada's other eTA eligibility rules.

Do not assume you qualify based only on having a US visa. Use the official Canadian eligibility checker.

CAN+ Processing

Some visitor visa applicants may also qualify for Canada's CAN+ processing approach.

IRCC states that an applicant may be eligible if they:

  • Held a Canadian visa during the previous 10 years, or
  • Hold a valid US non-immigrant visa

Eligible applications are automatically considered under CAN+. Applicants do not need to pay a separate CAN+ fee.

Common Problems That Can Weaken a Canada Visitor Visa Application

  • Unclear purpose of travel — Your reason should make sense when compared with trip duration, finances, employment, accommodation, invitation and personal circumstances.
  • Weak evidence that the trip is temporary — IRCC requires applicants to satisfy the officer that they will leave Canada at the end of their authorized stay.
  • Insufficient financial evidence — Your proposed trip should be financially realistic.
  • Incomplete documents — IRCC warns that incomplete visitor visa applications may be refused.
  • Inconsistent information — Dates, employment, travel history, family information and financial documents should not contradict one another.

Qatari Citizen vs Qatar Resident: The Difference

Qatari citizen — A Qatari passport holder travelling by air normally needs an eTA, not a visitor visa, following Canada's November 2025 policy change.

Qatar resident — A resident of Qatar holding another nationality needs to check the rules applying to the passport they will use. They may need a visitor visa, eTA or another travel document depending on their citizenship and circumstances.

Residence and citizenship are not the same thing.

Frequently Asked Questions About Applying for a Canada Visa From Qatar

Do Qatar citizens need a visa for Canada in 2026?

Generally, Qatari citizens travelling to Canada by air no longer need a traditional Canadian visitor visa. Since November 25, 2025, Qatari citizens normally need an eTA for air travel to Canada.

How much is the Canadian eTA for Qatar citizens?

The official Government of Canada eTA fee is CAN$7.

Do Qatar residents need a Canada visa?

It depends on their citizenship and passport. Being a Qatar resident does not by itself determine whether Canada requires a visa. Use Canada's official visa and eTA checker.

How much is a Canada visitor visa from Qatar?

The standard visitor visa government fee is CAN$100 per applicant.

Biometrics, where required, cost CAN$85 per individual.

How long does a Canada visa take from Qatar?

There is no fixed processing time that applies permanently. IRCC updates processing estimates regularly. Check the official processing-time tool shortly before and after applying.

Where do I give biometrics for a Canada visa in Qatar?

Biometrics can be provided at the Canada Visa Application Centre in Doha after IRCC issues a Biometric Instruction Letter and you book the required appointment.

How much bank balance is required for a Canada visitor visa from Qatar?

IRCC does not provide one fixed bank-balance amount that guarantees a standard visitor visa. Applicants need enough money for their stay based on their trip circumstances.

Is an invitation letter compulsory for a Canada visitor visa?

Not for every visitor. An invitation letter may be requested or useful when visiting family or friends, but IRCC states that having one does not guarantee approval.

Can I apply online for a Canada visa from Qatar?

Yes. Canada provides an online application process through IRCC. Applicants answer questions, upload required documents and pay applicable fees electronically.

Can I get a 10-year Canada visitor visa?

A visitor visa can generally be issued for up to 10 years, but the officer determines the actual validity. It cannot normally extend beyond relevant passport or biometrics validity.

Can I stay in Canada for 10 years if I receive a 10-year visa?

No. Visa validity and length of stay are different.

Most visitors are admitted for up to six months per visit unless the border officer specifies another period.

Does having a Canada visa guarantee entry?

No. A visa or eTA allows you to travel to Canada and request admission. The final entry decision is made at the Canadian border.

Final Thoughts

Applying to visit Canada from Qatar in 2026 is easier to understand once you separate citizenship from residence.

The most important change is that Qatari citizens became visa-exempt on November 25, 2025 and normally require an eTA when travelling to Canada by air.

People who live in Qatar but hold passports from other countries need to check whether their nationality requires a visitor visa, qualifies for an eTA, or falls under another Canadian travel-document rule.

For applicants who need a visitor visa, the basic process is:

Check eligibility → apply online → upload supporting documents → pay fees → complete biometrics if required → wait for IRCC → submit passport after approval → travel to Canada.

A strong application is not about submitting the largest number of documents. It is about presenting a clear, accurate and well-supported temporary travel purpose that meets Canada's immigration requirements.
