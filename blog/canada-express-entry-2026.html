What Is Express Entry?

Express Entry is an online application management system used by Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada, or IRCC, to manage applications from skilled workers seeking Canadian permanent residence.

Express Entry currently manages three federal immigration programs:

  1. Federal Skilled Worker Program, or FSWP
  2. Canadian Experience Class, or CEC
  3. Federal Skilled Trades Program, or FSTP

Candidates eligible for one of these programs can create an Express Entry profile and enter the pool. IRCC then ranks eligible candidates using the Comprehensive Ranking System, or CRS.

A portion of Canada's Provincial Nominee Program, or PNP, also operates through Express Entry. A qualifying provincial nomination can substantially increase a candidate's CRS score.

Is Express Entry Open in 2026?

Yes. Express Entry continues to operate in 2026.

IRCC conducts rounds of invitations throughout the year and invites eligible candidates from the Express Entry pool to apply for permanent residence.

However, entering the Express Entry pool does not guarantee an invitation.

Your chances depend on factors including:

  • Which Express Entry program you qualify for
  • Your CRS score
  • Your occupation
  • French-language ability
  • Canadian work experience
  • Provincial nomination
  • Whether you qualify for a category-based round
  • The type of invitation round IRCC conducts

This distinction matters because Canada increasingly uses category-based selection alongside traditional CRS ranking.

How Does Express Entry Work in 2026?

The process can be understood in five simple stages.

Stage 1: Check eligibility

You must qualify for at least one of the three federal programs managed through Express Entry.

Stage 2: Create an Express Entry profile

You provide information about your:

  • Age
  • Education
  • Language ability
  • Work experience
  • Marital status
  • Canadian experience
  • Family connections
  • Provincial nomination
  • Other relevant factors

Stage 3: Receive a CRS score

IRCC assigns your profile a Comprehensive Ranking System score.

Stage 4: Wait for a round of invitations

IRCC conducts different types of draws and invites eligible candidates.

Stage 5: Apply for permanent residence

If you receive an Invitation to Apply, or ITA, you currently have 60 days to submit your permanent residence application.

Express Entry Programs in Canada

Before thinking about your CRS score, first determine which Express Entry immigration program you qualify for.

This is important because program eligibility and CRS ranking are not the same thing.

A person can have a potentially competitive CRS profile but still need to meet the minimum requirements of an Express Entry program.

1. Federal Skilled Worker Program

The Federal Skilled Worker Program, or FSWP, is particularly relevant to skilled professionals who have qualifying work experience outside Canada.

For someone living and working in Qatar who has never worked in Canada, this is often the first Express Entry program worth checking.

To meet the core skilled-work requirement, your experience generally must:

  • Be in NOC TEER 0, 1, 2 or 3
  • Match your chosen primary occupation
  • Include the lead statement and most main duties of that NOC
  • Be paid work
  • Have been obtained during the previous 10 years
  • Include at least one year of continuous qualifying work, equivalent to 1,560 hours

Federal Skilled Worker Language Requirement

You need at least:

CLB 7 in all four language abilities

for your first official language.

The four abilities are:

  • Reading
  • Writing
  • Listening
  • Speaking

Federal Skilled Worker Education Requirement

You need at least secondary education.

If your education was completed outside Canada, you generally need an Educational Credential Assessment, or ECA, for immigration purposes.

The 67-Point Rule

The Federal Skilled Worker Program also has its own selection grid.

You need at least:

67 points out of 100

to qualify under the Federal Skilled Worker selection factors.

These points consider factors such as:

  • Age
  • Education
  • Language
  • Skilled work experience
  • Arranged employment
  • Adaptability

Important: 67 points are not your CRS score

This is a common misunderstanding.

The 67-point FSW grid determines whether you may qualify for the Federal Skilled Worker Program.

The CRS score determines how your Express Entry profile is ranked against other candidates.

They are two different scoring systems.

2. Canadian Experience Class

The Canadian Experience Class, or CEC, is designed for skilled workers who already have qualifying Canadian work experience.

Applicants generally need at least:

One year of eligible Canadian work experience during the previous three years.

The work must generally fall within:

  • TEER 0
  • TEER 1
  • TEER 2
  • TEER 3

The minimum language requirement depends on the occupation.

For TEER 0 or 1:

CLB/NCLC 7

For TEER 2 or 3:

CLB/NCLC 5

Unlike the Federal Skilled Worker Program, the Canadian Experience Class does not have a minimum education requirement.

3. Federal Skilled Trades Program

The Federal Skilled Trades Program is designed for people qualified in certain skilled trades.

Applicants generally need at least:

Two years of qualifying skilled-trade experience during the previous five years.

Eligible trades fall within specified NOC groups covering areas such as:

  • Technical trades
  • General trades
  • Natural resources
  • Agriculture-related production
  • Manufacturing
  • Processing
  • Cooks
  • Butchers
  • Bakers
  • Chefs

The applicant generally also needs either:

  • A valid full-time job offer for at least one year, or
  • A Canadian certificate of qualification in the skilled trade

Language requirements are lower than under the Federal Skilled Worker Program:

Speaking and listening: CLB/NCLC 5

Reading and writing: CLB/NCLC 4.

What Is the CRS Score?

The Comprehensive Ranking System, or CRS, is the points system IRCC uses to rank candidates in the Express Entry pool.

Your CRS score can be influenced by:

  • Age
  • Education
  • English ability
  • French ability
  • Canadian work experience
  • Foreign work experience
  • Spouse or partner qualifications
  • Canadian education
  • Sibling in Canada
  • Provincial nomination
  • Combinations of education, language and work experience

There is no permanent CRS score that guarantees an invitation.

The required cut-off can change from one invitation round to another.

Major 2026 Express Entry Change: Job Offers No Longer Give CRS Points

This is one of the most important changes for anyone reading older Express Entry guides.

Since March 25, 2025, IRCC no longer awards the previous CRS bonus points for job offers.

Previously, qualifying job offers could provide:

  • 50 CRS points for many skilled occupations
  • 200 CRS points for certain senior management positions

Those CRS points have been removed.

Does a Canadian Job Offer Still Matter?

Yes.

Removing job-offer CRS points does not mean Canadian job offers have become irrelevant.

A valid job offer may still matter for:

  • Eligibility under the Federal Skilled Worker Program
  • Eligibility under the Federal Skilled Trades Program
  • Certain Provincial Nominee Program streams
  • Employment opportunities after arriving in Canada

IRCC specifically states that candidates should continue providing job-offer information when it is relevant to program eligibility.

The correct statement for 2026 is therefore:

A Canadian job offer can still matter, but it no longer automatically gives the old 50 or 200 CRS points.

Express Entry Category-Based Selection in 2026

This is another major part of the modern Express Entry system.

Canada no longer selects candidates only through broad CRS-based rounds.

IRCC also runs category-based rounds targeting candidates with characteristics or experience Canada considers important to its economic goals.

As of August 2026, IRCC lists 10 Express Entry categories.

Current Express Entry Categories for 2026

1. French-Language Proficiency

Candidates with strong French-language skills can qualify for French-language category selection.

The current minimum is:

NCLC 7 in all four French-language abilities.

2. Healthcare and Social Services Occupations

This category includes qualifying occupations such as:

  • Doctors
  • Dentists
  • Pharmacists
  • Psychologists
  • Chiropractors
  • Other healthcare and social-service occupations listed by IRCC

For this occupation category, IRCC currently requires at least 12 months of qualifying work experience in a single listed occupation during the previous three years, which may have been obtained in Canada or abroad.

3. STEM Occupations

STEM means:

Science, Technology, Engineering and Mathematics

Candidates working in listed STEM occupations can be targeted through category-based invitations.

4. Trade Occupations

Canada continues to prioritize selected skilled-trade occupations through category-based Express Entry.

5. Education Occupations

Selected education professionals can qualify for category-based selection.

6. Transport Occupations

Transport became a major 2026 priority category and includes selected occupations such as pilots, aircraft mechanics and inspectors.

7. Physicians With Canadian Work Experience

Canada introduced a dedicated 2026 category for foreign medical doctors who have relevant Canadian work experience.

8. Senior Managers With Canadian Work Experience

Senior managers with qualifying Canadian work experience are another new category for 2026.

9. Researchers With Canadian Work Experience

Researchers with qualifying Canadian work experience are also included among the new 2026 categories.

10. Skilled Military Recruits

Canada introduced a category for highly skilled foreign military applicants recruited by the Canadian Armed Forces for selected roles.

Does Being in a Priority Occupation Guarantee PR?

No.

Being eligible for a category does not automatically provide Canadian permanent residence.

You must first:

  1. Qualify for one of the Express Entry programs
  2. Enter the Express Entry pool
  3. Meet the requirements for the relevant category
  4. Be sufficiently competitive within that category
  5. Receive an Invitation to Apply

IRCC still ranks candidates using CRS scores within category-based rounds.

This is why the statement:

"My occupation is on the category list, so I will get PR"

is incorrect.

Express Entry CRS Points in 2026

Your CRS score can reach a maximum of 1,200 points.

The system combines core human-capital factors, spouse factors, skill-transferability points and additional factors.

Core Factors

Important core factors include:

  • Age
  • Education
  • First official language
  • Second official language
  • Canadian work experience

For candidates without an accompanying spouse, the core human-capital section can provide up to 500 points. For candidates with an accompanying spouse or common-law partner, the applicant's core section is adjusted and spouse factors can contribute separately.

Age and CRS Points

Age can significantly affect Express Entry competitiveness.

Under the current CRS system, candidates aged 20 to 29 receive the maximum age points:

  • Up to 110 without an accompanying spouse
  • Up to 100 with an accompanying spouse

CRS age points begin declining from age 30 onward, reaching zero at age 45 or older.

This does not mean people over 45 cannot use Express Entry.

It means they receive no CRS points specifically for age and may need stronger scores in other areas such as:

  • Language
  • Education
  • French
  • Canadian experience
  • Provincial nomination

Education and CRS Points

Education can have a significant impact on your score.

For example, a candidate without an accompanying spouse can receive up to:

  • 120 core points for a bachelor's degree or qualifying three-year credential
  • 135 for a master's or qualifying professional degree
  • 150 for a doctorate

Foreign qualifications generally need to be supported by an Educational Credential Assessment to receive Express Entry education points.

Language Can Make a Major Difference to CRS

Language is one of the most important Express Entry factors.

IRCC accepts these English tests for Express Entry:

  • IELTS General Training
  • CELPIP-General
  • PTE Core

For French:

  • TEF Canada
  • TCF Canada

IELTS Academic is not the Express Entry IELTS test

For Express Entry, IRCC requires IELTS General Training, not IELTS Academic.

IRCC also states that IELTS One Skill Retake is not accepted for Express Entry.

How Long Are Language Test Results Valid?

Your language results must be:

Less than two years old

when you:

  • Complete your Express Entry profile
  • Submit your permanent residence application

If the results expire before you submit the PR application, IRCC can refuse the application.

Candidates should therefore monitor test expiry dates carefully while waiting in the Express Entry pool.

Why CLB 9 Is Important

Meeting the minimum language requirement only makes you eligible.

It does not necessarily make you competitive.

For example, under the Federal Skilled Worker Program, CLB 7 is the minimum.

But reaching CLB 9 or higher can have a much larger impact because language scores interact with education and foreign work experience through the CRS skill-transferability system.

For candidates applying from Qatar with strong foreign education and work experience, improving language scores can therefore be one of the most practical ways to increase CRS competitiveness.

French Can Be Especially Valuable in 2026

French has become increasingly important within Express Entry.

Candidates who achieve at least NCLC 7 in all four French abilities can potentially receive additional CRS points.

Depending on their English results, French can currently provide up to:

50 additional CRS points.

Strong French can also make the candidate eligible for the dedicated French-language proficiency category.

This means French can potentially help in two ways:

Higher CRS + category-based selection eligibility

Educational Credential Assessment for Express Entry

If your degree, diploma or certificate was earned outside Canada, you may need an Educational Credential Assessment.

An ECA shows how your foreign education compares with a Canadian credential.

For the Federal Skilled Worker Program, foreign-educated principal applicants generally need an ECA for eligibility.

Candidates under other Express Entry programs can also use an ECA to claim CRS points for foreign education.

How Long Is an ECA Valid?

An Express Entry ECA must be:

Less than five years old

when you create your Express Entry profile and when you submit the permanent residence application.

IRCC-designated organizations currently include bodies such as:

  • World Education Services
  • Comparative Education Service
  • International Credential Assessment Service of Canada
  • International Qualifications Assessment Service
  • International Credential Evaluation Service

Some regulated professions have specific professional bodies for credential assessments.

What Is NOC and TEER?

Canada uses the National Occupational Classification, or NOC, to classify jobs.

The system also uses TEER, meaning:

Training, Education, Experience and Responsibilities.

Choosing the correct NOC is important.

For Federal Skilled Worker eligibility, the duties you actually performed must substantially match the NOC's lead statement and most main duties.

The job title alone is not enough.

For example, calling yourself a "manager" does not automatically make a management NOC correct if your real responsibilities do not match that occupation.

Your NOC should reflect what you actually did at work, not which NOC produces the best immigration outcome.

Provincial Nominee Program and Express Entry

A Provincial Nominee Program can be extremely important for a candidate with a lower CRS score.

If an Express Entry candidate receives a qualifying provincial or territorial nomination, the current CRS system provides:

600 additional points.

That can transform a profile.

Provinces operate their own nomination streams based on their:

  • Labour-market needs
  • Occupations
  • Skills
  • Regional priorities
  • Employer needs
  • Candidate profiles

If your CRS is not competitive for federal selection, checking appropriate PNP streams may be worthwhile.

However, provincial programs have their own eligibility requirements, and a nomination should align with a genuine intention to live in the nominating province or territory.

Can You Apply for Express Entry From Qatar?

Yes.

Express Entry is an online system, and eligible skilled workers living outside Canada can create a profile.

For example, a professional legally residing and working in Qatar may potentially qualify under the Federal Skilled Worker Program based on foreign work experience, education, language ability and other eligibility factors.

Your Qatar residence itself does not provide extra CRS points simply because you live in Qatar.

Your profile is assessed according to Canada's Express Entry rules.

Documents Needed Before Creating an Express Entry Profile

It is useful to prepare certain documents before creating the profile.

Depending on your situation, these can include:

  • Passport
  • Approved language-test results
  • Educational Credential Assessment
  • NOC information
  • Employment history
  • Provincial nomination, if applicable
  • Canadian job-offer details, if applicable
  • Proof-of-funds information

IRCC recommends preparing key Express Entry documents before creating the profile.

How to Apply for Canada Express Entry in 2026

Here is the process step by step.

Step 1: Check Which Express Entry Program You Qualify For

Determine whether you meet the requirements of:

  • Federal Skilled Worker Program
  • Canadian Experience Class
  • Federal Skilled Trades Program

IRCC provides an official eligibility questionnaire to help candidates determine whether they meet Express Entry requirements.

Step 2: Take an Approved Language Test

You must take an approved English or French language test even if English or French is your first language.

Accepted tests include:

English

  • IELTS General Training
  • CELPIP-General
  • PTE Core

French

  • TEF Canada
  • TCF Canada

Do not create your profile using estimated IELTS scores.

You need valid official results.

Step 3: Obtain an ECA if Required

If your education was completed outside Canada, obtain the required Educational Credential Assessment before claiming points for it.

The ECA must be for immigration purposes and from an IRCC-designated organization or professional body.

Step 4: Identify Your Correct NOC

Find the NOC that genuinely matches your work history.

Check:

  • Lead statement
  • Main duties
  • TEER level

For Federal Skilled Worker eligibility, your primary occupation does not necessarily have to be your most recent occupation, but the qualifying work must meet IRCC's requirements.

Step 5: Calculate Your CRS Score

Before creating the profile, use the official IRCC CRS calculator to estimate your score.

Remember that the calculator is for guidance and the final score is generated from the information in your actual Express Entry profile.

Step 6: Create Your Express Entry Profile

Complete the Express Entry questionnaire in your IRCC secure account.

If you meet the minimum criteria of at least one Express Entry program, IRCC places your profile in the pool.

Once in the pool, your profile receives a CRS score.

Step 7: Keep Your Profile Updated

Your Express Entry information must remain accurate.

Update it when important circumstances change, such as:

  • New language results
  • Marriage
  • Divorce
  • New child
  • New education
  • New work experience
  • Provincial nomination
  • Change in employment
  • Expiring documents

An Express Entry profile normally remains active for 12 months. If you are not invited during that period, it expires and you can create another profile if you remain eligible.

Step 8: Wait for an Invitation to Apply

IRCC conducts rounds of invitations throughout the year.

These can include:

  • Program-specific rounds
  • Category-based rounds
  • Other Express Entry rounds authorized by IRCC

Candidates meeting the criteria for a particular round are ranked according to their CRS scores.

There is no guarantee that simply entering the pool will result in an ITA.

Step 9: Receive an Invitation to Apply

If selected, IRCC sends an Invitation to Apply for permanent residence.

You currently have:

60 days

to submit the complete Express Entry permanent residence application.

Do not wait until the final week to begin collecting documents.

Some records, especially police certificates, can take time.

Step 10: Complete Your Immigration Medical Exam

This is important because older guides may describe the timing differently.

For Express Entry permanent residence, IRCC currently instructs applicants to obtain an immigration medical examination after receiving an Invitation to Apply but before submitting the permanent residence application.

The examination must be completed by an IRCC-approved panel physician.

Your normal family doctor cannot perform the official immigration medical examination unless they are an approved panel physician.

Step 11: Obtain Police Certificates

Express Entry applicants may need police certificates from countries where they have lived.

IRCC advises candidates to begin obtaining certificates early because they can take time.

For the country where you currently live, the police certificate normally needs to have been issued no more than six months before you submit the application.

For another country, IRCC generally requires a certificate issued after the last time you lived there for six consecutive months or longer.

For Qatar residents, this means your immigration history may require certificates from Qatar and other countries where you previously lived, depending on IRCC's rules and your individual history.

Step 12: Prepare Proof of Funds if Required

Settlement funds are required for applicants who need them under:

  • Federal Skilled Worker Program
  • Federal Skilled Trades Program

You generally do not need proof of funds if:

  • You are applying under the Canadian Experience Class, or
  • You are authorized to work in Canada and have a qualifying valid job offer, even if applying under FSWP or FSTP

What counts as proof of funds?

IRCC generally requires official bank or financial-institution letters showing information including:

  • Your name
  • Account numbers
  • Opening dates
  • Current balances
  • Average balances for the previous six months
  • Outstanding debts

You cannot use borrowed money as settlement funds.

You also cannot use equity in real estate as proof of settlement funds.

Step 13: Upload the Complete Permanent Residence Application

Once invited, IRCC gives you a personalized document checklist.

Documents can include:

  • Passport
  • Language results
  • ECA
  • Employment reference letters
  • Police certificates
  • Medical examination evidence
  • Proof of funds
  • Marriage certificate, if applicable
  • Birth certificates for dependent children
  • Provincial nomination documents
  • Other personal or program-specific evidence

The information in the documents must support the claims made in your Express Entry profile.

How Long Does Express Entry Take in 2026?

There are two different timelines people often confuse.

Time Waiting in the Express Entry Pool

There is no fixed waiting period.

You may:

  • Receive an invitation quickly
  • Remain in the pool for months
  • Never receive an ITA before the profile expires

This depends on your CRS score and the invitation rounds conducted by IRCC.

Permanent Residence Processing After ITA

IRCC's current processing information uses approximately six months as the service standard/reference point for many permanent residence applications, though individual cases can take longer.

The processing period begins after IRCC receives a complete permanent residence application, not when you first create the Express Entry profile.

How Can You Improve Your CRS Score in 2026?

There is no single method that works for every candidate, but several areas can materially affect CRS competitiveness.

1. Improve Your Language Score

Language can provide both direct CRS points and skill-transferability points.

Moving from minimum eligibility levels toward CLB 9 or CLB 10 can substantially change some profiles.

2. Learn French

Strong French can:

  • Provide additional CRS points
  • Make you eligible for French category-based draws

3. Obtain a Provincial Nomination

A qualifying nomination currently provides:

600 CRS points.

4. Increase Skilled Work Experience

Additional qualifying foreign or Canadian experience can improve parts of your CRS profile, depending on your circumstances.

5. Improve Education

Additional recognized education may improve both human-capital and skill-transferability points.

Foreign credentials normally need a valid ECA to generate education points.

6. Include Your Spouse Strategically

When applying with an accompanying spouse or common-law partner, their:

  • Education
  • Language ability
  • Canadian work experience

can contribute CRS points.

Do not change accompanying status artificially simply to manipulate points. The information in your immigration application must accurately reflect your real family circumstances.

What Is a Good CRS Score in 2026?

There is no permanent number that can honestly be called a guaranteed good CRS score.

IRCC cut-offs vary by:

  • Date
  • Type of invitation round
  • Number of invitations
  • Category
  • Candidate pool
  • Program

A score that is insufficient for one round may be competitive in another category-based or program-specific round.

Therefore, applicants should compare their score with current official IRCC invitation rounds, not with an old blog article claiming that a fixed CRS number guarantees PR.

Is 400 CRS Enough for Canada PR?

Possibly in certain circumstances, but 400 is not automatically sufficient.

A candidate with a lower CRS could potentially improve their position through:

  • Provincial nomination
  • French category eligibility
  • Priority occupation category
  • Better language scores
  • Canadian experience
  • Additional education

A provincial nomination alone currently adds 600 CRS points.

Is 500 CRS Enough for Express Entry?

There is no guaranteed threshold.

A CRS score of 500 can be competitive in some rounds and insufficient in others.

IRCC publishes the lowest CRS score invited for each official round, so candidates should follow the latest invitation data directly rather than relying on a permanent number.

Can You Get Canada PR Without a Job Offer in 2026?

Yes.

A Canadian job offer is not mandatory for:

  • Canadian Experience Class
  • Federal Skilled Worker Program

although job offers can still affect certain program requirements or provincial pathways.

And as noted earlier, job offers no longer provide the old 50 or 200 CRS bonus points.

Therefore:

No Canadian job offer does not automatically mean no Express Entry eligibility.

Can You Apply for Express Entry Without IELTS?

You cannot use Express Entry without an approved language test.

But IELTS is not your only English option.

IRCC currently accepts:

  • CELPIP-General
  • IELTS General Training
  • PTE Core

For French, it accepts:

  • TEF Canada
  • TCF Canada

So the accurate answer is:

You can apply without IELTS specifically, but you cannot apply without an approved language test.

Can You Create Express Entry Before Getting IELTS Results?

You need valid approved language-test results to complete an eligible Express Entry profile.

IRCC requires candidates to enter their actual test results into the profile.

Do not enter estimated scores.

Can You Apply Without an ECA?

It depends on your education and Express Entry program.

If you completed education outside Canada and are applying as the principal applicant under the Federal Skilled Worker Program, an ECA is generally required.

An ECA is also needed if you want CRS education points for foreign qualifications.

Canadian credentials do not need an ECA.

Does Express Entry Guarantee a Job in Canada?

No.

Express Entry is an immigration system.

It is not a recruitment service.

Receiving permanent residence does not mean IRCC gives you employment.

Candidates are responsible for their own job search and professional licensing where applicable.

IRCC also notes that an ECA does not guarantee employment or professional licensing in Canada.

Does Express Entry Apply to Quebec?

Express Entry is generally designed for candidates intending to live outside Quebec.

Quebec selects its own economic immigrants through separate immigration programs. The Federal Skilled Worker Program specifically requires applicants to plan to live outside Quebec.

Applicants whose real destination is Quebec should investigate Quebec immigration programs instead of presenting another province as their intended destination.

Common Express Entry Mistakes to Avoid

Choosing the Wrong NOC

Your duties should match your chosen NOC, not merely your job title.

Using IELTS Academic

For Express Entry, IRCC accepts IELTS General Training, not IELTS Academic.

Letting Language Results Expire

Language tests must still be valid when the permanent residence application is submitted.

Letting the ECA Expire

An ECA must be less than five years old when the profile and application are submitted.

Assuming a Job Offer Gives CRS Points

The old job-offer bonus was removed in March 2025.

Borrowing Settlement Funds

IRCC does not allow borrowed money to be used as settlement funds.

Claiming Work Experience You Cannot Prove

Employment records need to support the work history claimed in the profile.

Thinking a Category Guarantees an Invitation

Category eligibility still requires Express Entry eligibility and sufficient ranking within the relevant selection round.

What is the minimum CRS score for Express Entry in 2026?

There is no single fixed minimum CRS score that guarantees an invitation. Cut-off scores vary by invitation round.

What IELTS score is required for Express Entry?

It depends on the program.

For the Federal Skilled Worker Program, the minimum first-language requirement is CLB 7 in all four abilities.

CEC requirements are CLB 7 for TEER 0 or 1 and CLB 5 for TEER 2 or 3.

Federal Skilled Trades requires CLB 5 in speaking/listening and CLB 4 in reading/writing.

Can I apply for Express Entry from Qatar?

Yes. Eligible foreign skilled workers can create an Express Entry profile while living outside Canada, including in Qatar.

Do I need a job offer for Express Entry?

Not for every program.

A job offer is not required for CEC or FSW eligibility, although it can remain relevant in certain immigration contexts.

Does a job offer increase CRS in 2026?

Not through the old job-offer bonus. IRCC removed the 50 and 200 job-offer CRS points on March 25, 2025.

How long is an Express Entry profile valid?

An Express Entry profile generally expires after 12 months if you do not receive an invitation.

How long do I get after receiving an ITA?

You currently have 60 days to submit your permanent residence application after receiving an Invitation to Apply.

How much money do I need for Express Entry?

It depends on your family size and whether your program requires proof of funds.

The current published minimum for one person is CAD $15,263, while a family of four requires CAD $28,362.

How much does Express Entry cost in 2026?

After the April 30, 2026 fee increase, a principal Federal High Skilled applicant currently pays CAD $990 processing fee plus CAD $600 Right of Permanent Residence Fee, for a total of CAD $1,590, excluding biometrics and third-party costs.

Do I need a medical exam for Express Entry?

Yes. Express Entry permanent residence applicants are currently instructed to complete an upfront immigration medical exam after receiving their ITA and before submitting the PR application.

Is French useful for Express Entry?

Yes. Strong French can provide up to 50 additional CRS points and may make a candidate eligible for French-language category selection.

How many CRS points does a provincial nomination give?

A qualifying provincial or territorial nomination currently provides 600 additional CRS points.

Can Express Entry guarantee permanent residence?

No.

Entering the Express Entry pool only makes you available for selection. You still need to receive an Invitation to Apply and satisfy immigration and admissibility requirements.
